# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are documented in this file. The format is based on
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the mod follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added

- A pixel art icon, shown in Mod Menu, in NeoForge's mod list and at the top of the README.

### Changed

- The description in the mod list names all features.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

First release, for Fabric and NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21.1 – 26.3.

### Added

- Cold start: crouch on the ground with an elytra on to gather strength for one mighty wing beat. The air stirs at
  your feet, feathers drift off the charged wings, and releasing crouch throws you 16 blocks into the air in a gust of
  wind and feathers.
- Endless flight: diving regains 50 % more speed, so diving and pulling up again keeps a flight going forever.
- Wing flaps: crouch while flying to flap the wings and speed up like with a weak firework rocket, for 3 seconds at a
  time with a 5 second cooldown.
- The wings rise while charging, strike down for the cold start and beat while flapping, for every player with the
  mod on their client.
- `config/elytrabuff.properties`, reloaded automatically and editable in game through NeoForge's mod list or Mod Menu,
  with range checks and resetting values to their defaults.
